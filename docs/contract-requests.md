`/v1/auth/resident/*` and (R2) `/v1/technician/*` only. Each is mocked in `mocks/contract-requests.ts` and marked
`// CONTRACT-REQUEST` where it is called; delete the mock when the contract ships it (`pnpm gen:api --adopt`).
